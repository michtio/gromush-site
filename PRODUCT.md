## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS generated by a stdlib Python build from one data source in the repo, hosted on GitHub Pages for now; no CMS, CRM, analytics or cookies; forms send plain email through a form service. See `specs/2026-10-19-gromush-site/plan.md` (decided 2026-11-03).

## Users

Two primary buyer groups, weighted equally (confirmed 2026-10-19):

- **Professional kitchens and food businesses** in the delivery region: chefs and restaurant owners (the most frequent), large kitchens (hospitals, care homes, schools, company restaurants, caterers, hotels), and speciality shops (greengrocers, bakers, butchers/traiteurs, delis, organic shops). They are buying an ingredient: they want taste, texture, unusual varieties, reliable local delivery and a risk-free way to try.
- **Home cooks and families** in the same region who want fresh, local, unusual mushrooms for their own kitchen, and who may join the MushLove community (following the grow, adopting a bucket, workshops).

All of them are in Knokke-Heist, Damme, Sluis (NL), Brugge, Blankenberge and surroundings. They are often on a phone.

## Product Purpose

GroMush sells fresh oyster mushrooms that Anthony Watteeuw grows himself, in Knokke-Heist (Westkapelle). The website exists to sell them: it turns a visitor into a request (a free tasting box, an order, a volume conversation, a bucket adoption) that reaches Anthony, who confirms personally. Success is qualified requests from professionals and consumers in the delivery region, and the colour and character of the varieties making people want to cook with them.

## Positioning

The grower is the seller. Six oyster varieties in colours most people have never seen (yellow, pink, blue-grey, white, grey, Italian), grown in 20-litre straw buckets a few kilometres from the kitchens they go to, harvested and delivered by the same person and delivered personally. No wholesaler, no long chain. A neighbouring supplier cannot truthfully claim the grower, the place and the hand-delivery.

## Operating Context

- **Ordering is a request, not a checkout.** Visitors ask for a free tasting box (professionals in the region), an order, a volume conversation or an adoption; Anthony follows up by email, phone or WhatsApp and confirms. There is no cart, account or online payment (confirmed 2026-10-19).
- **Indoor growing, four phases:** straw soaked in cold water and drained; layered with spawn in 20L buckets; incubated in the dark; fruited in a humid, lit grow room. Then harvest, packing in breathable crates, and personal delivery.
- **Outdoor log growing in a food forest does not exist yet.** It is a plan for 2027 and is presented only as a plan, never as existing, adoptable or harvestable.
- Packs of 250 g, 500 g, 1 kg and larger on request. Availability follows the harvest. Keeps at least a week at 4 °C, not airtight.
- Dutch (nl-BE) language, addressed as "je/jouw".

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Varieties: gele (Pleurotus citrinopileatus), roze (P. djamor), witte (P. ostreatus), blauwe (P. ostreatus var. columbinus), Italiaanse (P. pulmonarius), grijze (P. ostreatus). All six are sold; canonical taste lines are in the PRD (Q10).
- Offers: the free "ProefBox" for professionals in the region; the paid "ChefBox" for chefs; large-kitchen supply in a start-up phase (large volumes not yet guaranteed); bucket adoption as a request; home-cook orders for pick-up. No prices on the site.
- Never on the site: prices, discounts, volumes, organic claims (certification in progress), a street address (only "Westkapelle"), testimonials. Phone and WhatsApp 0499 24 23 59 may be published.
- Full requirements and the consolidated sitemap: `specs/2026-10-19-gromush-site/prd.md` and `sitemap.md`.

- Official baseline: **"Ambachtelijk gekweekt."** Core line: **"Van kwekerij tot bord."** Anthony signs every email "MushLove", which is also the community's name.

## Product Principles

1. Sell the mushroom first: colour, texture and taste make people want it; the story supports the sale, it does not replace it.
2. Every page ends in one clear request that reaches Anthony.
3. Honest over impressive: no invented prices, claims, reviews or availability.
4. Equal doors for professionals and home cooks, each spoken to in their own terms.
5. Local and personal is the advantage: the grower, the place and the delivery are visible.
